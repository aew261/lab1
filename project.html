PROJECT RES HUB

Our project is based on the observation that students often choose residence blindly without knowing living conditions and infrastructure, they only see the condition on arrival. Our webapp aims to solve this by providig a platform where students who lived in these residences provided by the school to give an honest review so other students can choose wisely the residences.
